# Recorded vocabularies

Gesture templates exported from the demo's **Export JSON**, ready for
**Import JSON** on the Teach gestures tab. Import merges into whatever is
already recorded; it never replaces.

| file | gestures | takes | recorded by |
|---|---|---|---|
| `poyen.json` | 23 | 26 | the author, 2026-08-29, webcam at ~1 m |

**These are one person's gestures.** Recognition compares against the takes
in the file, so they match the person who recorded them well and other
people poorly (measured 42% cross-person). Use this file to try the demo
without recording first; then record your own takes on top of it — three per
gesture — and they will take over.
